ISE 8110 · Blocking game

The Popcorn Problem

Campus Dining wants to stock one popcorn brand in every residence hall. The goal: the fewest unpopped kernels per bag.

  • Response: number of unpopped kernels left in the bag (lower is better).
  • The catch: every residence hall has its own microwave. Some are new, some are older, and their power differs.
  • Budget: 20 bags. Each microwave can pop 4 bags before the testing window closes.
How we play: each group answers on paper. When every group is ready, we reveal on screen. Five rounds: Diagnose, Design, Analyze, Random Microwaves, and Why Microwaves Differ.

Round 1 · The roommate test

Four roommates each popped one brand in the microwave on their own floor, then counted the unpopped kernels.

Group task 1. Do you agree that Brand B is the best brand?
2. Name two reasons Brand B could look best even if it is not the best brand.
  • Brand and microwave are tangled together. Each brand was popped in a different microwave, so a difference in kernels could come from the brand, the microwave, or both. We cannot separate them.
  • One bag per brand. With no replication, we have no estimate of bag-to-bag noise.
Keep Brand B and Microwave 2 in mind. We come back to them in Round 3.

Round 2 · Pick the plan

Dining proposes three ways to use the 20 bags. Each column is one microwave (4 bags). Letters are brands.

Group task 1. Which plan would you approve?
2. For each plan you reject, write one sentence on what goes wrong.
  • Plan 1 (fully random): random, but by chance some microwaves never see some brands. Microwave differences end up mixed into the error.
  • Plan 2 (one brand per microwave): the same problem as Round 1. Brand and microwave cannot be separated.
  • Plan 3 (RCBD): every brand is popped once in every microwave, so brands are compared within the same microwave.
Blocks: microwaves. Complete: all 4 brands in each microwave. Randomized: the popping order is random inside each microwave. Click below to randomize the order for Plan 3.

What we learned

  • Round 1: when each brand gets its own microwave, brand and microwave effects cannot be separated.
  • Round 2: an RCBD puts every treatment in every block and randomizes within each block.
